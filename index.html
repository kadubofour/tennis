FAQ

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp 054 902 9200.

When and where are sessions?

Every Saturday and Sunday at Rolider Sports Complex. Kids play 2:30 to 4:00 PM and adults play 3:00 to 5:00 PM.

How much does it cost?

A walk-in group session is GH₵ 250, and a Monthly Membership is GH₵ 1,500. Private Lessons with a coach are GH₵ 500 per session, and one-on-one sessions with the head coach are GH₵ 1,000. For kids' rates, call or WhatsApp 054 902 9200.

I've never played. Can I join?

Yes, absolutely. Our weekend sessions are open to every level, including complete beginners. Your coach will start with the basics like grip, footwork and getting the ball over the net, and adjust drills to your level so you're never out of your depth. If you'd like a head start, a Private Lesson is a great way to learn the fundamentals one-on-one.

Can parents play while kids train?

Yes! Kids' and adults' sessions run at the same time on the same days, so the whole family can come together.

Do I need my own racket?

There's no need to buy one before you've tried it. Just mention it when you book on WhatsApp and we'll help you get set up for your first session. Once you're hooked, your coach can recommend a racket that suits your age, size and playing style.

What should I bring?

Comfortable sportswear, tennis shoes or trainers with flat soles, a water bottle, and sun protection such as a cap and sunscreen, since sessions are outdoors in the afternoon. If you have a racket, bring it along too.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp 054 902 9200, or fill in the booking form below and it'll open WhatsApp with your details ready to send.
